ABOUT LOANIQ

Built for clearer loan assessment

LoanIQ combines a trained machine-learning pipeline with a guided assessment interface.

L

From raw features to a usable decision experience.

LoanIQ accepts applicant, financial and credit information, preprocesses the data and sends it through the trained prediction pipeline.

The interface is designed to keep technical model details behind the scenes while presenting the information in a way that is easier for a person to understand and review.
