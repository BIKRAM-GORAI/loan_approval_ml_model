📖 Field Guide & Instructions

💡 How It Works

Enter applicant details in the form or pick any pre-configured Sample Applicant, then click Predict Loan Approval. The Machine Learning pipeline will preprocess the features, apply scaling, and evaluate the application to return an approval decision with its probability score.

📋 Feature & Label Descriptions

👤 Applicant Information

  • Age: Age in years (18–100). Reflects financial maturity and career stage.
  • Annual Income ($): Total gross annual income. Higher earnings indicate higher repayment capacity.
  • Credit Score: FICO score (300–850). Scores above 700 signify strong credit reliability.
  • Employment Status: Current status (Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed). Stable employment strongly supports approval.
  • Education Level: Highest completed level of education (High School to Doctorate).

💰 Loan Information

  • Requested Loan Amount ($): Total principal amount requested. Larger amounts relative to income carry higher risk.
  • Loan Duration (months): Loan repayment term (e.g. 12, 36, 60 months).
  • Marital Status: Legal marital status (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed).
  • Number of Dependents: Count of individuals financially supported by the applicant.
  • Home Ownership: Current housing situation (Own, Mortgage, Rent, Other).

📊 Financial & Credit History

  • Monthly Debt Payments ($): Total ongoing recurring monthly payments (credit cards, existing loans, auto payments).
  • Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio: Monthly debt divided by gross monthly income (e.g. 0.20 = 20%). Ratios under 0.36 are considered healthy.
  • Bankruptcy History: Whether the applicant has ever filed for bankruptcy (0 = No, 1 = Yes).
  • Previous Loan Defaults: Whether the applicant ever failed to repay a loan previously (0 = No, 1 = Yes).
  • Payment History Score: Track record score of on-time payments (0–30+). Higher scores mean reliable repayment history.
